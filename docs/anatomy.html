Signature

  1. 1White page, cream cards

    Figures on white (charcoal in the dark twin); panels and tiles in cream.

  2. 2Claim title

    A sentence with a verb, in Palatino. Other text in a humanist sans.

  3. 3Six families, fixed order

    Tones for series, tints for areas, deeps for emphasis.

  4. 4Direct labels

    A ringed dot and an ink label at each line end. Legend only for five or six series; above six, facet.

  5. 5Hairline grid, no frame

    Horizontal hairlines only; no box or side spines.

  6. 6Unit line

    Quantity and unit above the y axis, upright and muted.

  7. 7Traceable footer

    Source, script and git commit, bottom left, muted.

Typefaces

Title face

Palatino

A claim in Palatino reads at arm’s length and from two metres away

For the claim title and slide or poster headings only.

Windows: Palatino Linotype · LaTeX: TeX Gyre Pagella · web: 'Palatino Linotype', 'TeX Gyre Pagella', Palatino, serif

Body face

Corbel

parts per million · 1958 · 2020 · γ = 0.5 · −3 °C · 12,045 deaths

For all other text.

Windows: Corbel · LaTeX and the web: Lato · fallback: Segoe UI, then DejaVu Sans

One weight per face; italics only for maths variables (VA).

Tables use lining figures: font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums, \usepackage[lining]{lato}.

Type scale

Draw at print size; do not rescale.

Text is ink or muted; a label on a tint uses the deep step.

Writing

  1. Sentence case; no full stop after a title or label.
  2. Title: a claim with a verb.
  3. Unit line: a noun phrase, such as "parts per million".
  4. True minus (U+2212); a space before units: 10 km, 5 %.
  5. Greek as letters, thin spaces around "=".
  6. No internal abbreviations: "generations", not "gens".

If a colleague cannot state the claim within three seconds with the title covered, remove a series or split a panel.

Specimen

  1. Title block: flush with the plot's left edge; the subtitle gives data set, sample and key settings.
  2. Panel title: a bold letter and a 9 pt claim in ink.
  3. Axes: three to five labels at round numbers.
  4. In a journal: the claim moves to the caption, the footer to the data statement.

Marks

Stacked fills and heat-map cells: 1.2 pt surface-colour gaps, no outlines.

In a paper: lines 1.2 pt, member lines 0.6 pt, markers 20 pt² with a 1.0 pt ring.

Labels

  1. Line labels: ink, 7.5 pt, 6 pt right of the end dot; colliding labels spread with a 0.5 pt muted leader (endlabels in vellum.py, endLabels in vellum-web.js).
  2. Callouts: ink text, thin muted leader, no arrowhead.
  3. Reference lines: label at the right end, above, muted.
  4. Quiet notes: muted, 7.5 pt, inside the plot away from the data.
  5. Values on marks only when the value is the point.
  6. Legend: top left inside the plot, no frame, end labels kept.

Multi-panel figures

Panels in reading order, the finding last. Shared axes share ticks; one unit line per row. Gaps: 0.42 of a panel width, 0.55 of a panel height.

Small multiples: same axes, one family per panel, the rest in rule grey behind.
