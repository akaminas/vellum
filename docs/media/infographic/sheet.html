Life expectancy at birth, country by country1950–2023 · 172 countries on the map
Countries have drawn together: the middle 80 % spanned 30 years of life expectancy in 1950, 19 in 2023
In 1950 the median country could expect 49.2 years of life at birth; in 2023, 75.3. The countries that started lowest gained most, so the dots crowd upward. The pandemic dented almost every country at once: 203 of 236 fell in 2020 or 2021, most of all in Latin America.
