−1.7yearsworld life expectancy at birth, 2019 to 2021, after seven decades of gains
The pandemic brought world life expectancy its deepest fall since 1960
From 46.4 years in 1950, the world gained 26.2 years of life expectancy by 2019. Two pandemic years took 1.7 of them back, to the level of 2012. By 2023 the loss was made up, at 73.2 years, a record.
