Interactive figures

Each figure is complete as a static image; tooltips, crosshairs and highlights are added on top. All follow the light or dark theme.

3D surface and flat twin

A two-peak surface, shaded by height on the lagoon gradient. Drag to rotate it; point at the flat twin to mark the same spot on the surface (an open ring when hidden). The view starts orthographic at elevation 24°, azimuth −58°; "Reset view" returns there.

Life expectancy explorer

Choose up to four countries; each keeps its colour while selected, and the world average sits behind in grey. The crosshair reads every line for a year, and the title is recomputed for the selection.

Progressive reveal

Life expectancy by income group, built one series per step as in a talk, with fixed axes; step 5 highlights the claim's group in terracotta. Press 1, then Next or the arrow keys.

Heat map

Disaster deaths by type and decade, 1900s to 2010s, on a log-scaled lagoon gradient; hatched cells have no record. Point at the cells, or tab in and use the arrow keys; rows can be sorted by total.

Penguin scatter

Pick any two of four penguin measures; each species gets a fit line labelled with its correlation, and the title is recomputed. Beak length against depth shows Simpson's paradox, with the pooled fit as a dotted grey line.

World map

Life expectancy in five teal classes fixed at the 2023 quintiles; use the slider to move between 1950 and 2023. A linked dot strip below shows small countries. Missing values are hatched in terracotta.

Network

Character co-occurrence in a static, pre-computed force layout; the six largest groups take the six tones. Hover, tap or search a character to highlight it and its neighbours in terracotta.

Colour tools

The three tools from the colour page, computed live from vellum.json with the same formulas as the Python generators. The simulator shows the palette under each colour-vision deficiency beside a common default palette, two of whose colours fall to 0.7 apart under protanopia. The gradient inspector plots lightness along each gradient, including a rainbow for comparison.

Build. Plain JavaScript modules on vellum-web.js, which reads the same vellum.json as the other tools. No framework or chart library. Each figure has a table view for readers who do not use a pointer.
