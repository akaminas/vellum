3D surface and flat twin

A two-peak surface and its flat twin. Drag to rotate; point at the twin to mark the same spot on the surface.

Life expectancy explorer

Choose up to four countries against the world average; the crosshair reads every line for a year.

Progressive reveal

Life expectancy by income group, one series per step. Press 1, then Next or the arrow keys.

Heat map

Disaster deaths by type and decade; hatched cells have no record. Point at a cell or use the arrow keys; sort rows by total.

Penguin scatter

Pick any two of four penguin measures; each species gets its own fit. Beak length against depth shows Simpson's paradox.

World map

Life expectancy in five classes fixed at the 2023 quintiles. Move the slider from 1950 to 2023.

Network

Character co-occurrence. Hover, tap or search a character to highlight its neighbours.

Colour tools

The colour page tools, computed live from vellum.json: a colour-vision simulator, a gradient inspector and a palette explorer.
