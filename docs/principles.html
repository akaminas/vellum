Recipe

  1. Write the claim in one sentence; it is the title.
  2. Pick the form by the job of the data. Chart forms.
  3. Pick the medium and take its sizes. Media.
  4. Assign tones in slot order, six at most.
  5. Build the anatomy: title, unit line, end labels, hairline grid, footer. Anatomy.
  6. Run the checklist and look at the render. Checklist.
  7. Save PDF for print and PNG at 300 dpi for screens.

Removal test

Remove the element or make it one flat colour. If no information is lost, delete it.

  • a gradient background
  • 3D on 2D data
  • a glow, shadow, bevel or reflection
  • an outline on a bar or dot
  • a box around the plot
  • a legend for four series or fewer

Never

  • a dashed gridline
  • a rainbow scale
  • a pie with more than six slices
  • a noun-phrase title
  • text in a series colour
  • a seventh series colour
  • a circular form unless the angle means something
  • 3D axes, except a surface or 3D scatter with a 2D twin

References

  • Machado, G. M., Oliveira, M. M. and Fernandes, L. A. F. (2009). A physiologically-based model for simulation of color vision deficiency. IEEE TVCG 15(6), 1291–1298.
