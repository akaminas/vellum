About

Have you ever sat behind the screen for hours, deciding on the details of a plot? Which colours, which font, where the legend goes. Vellum makes those decisions once, each with a written reason, so your hours go into the claim instead. It is a style, not a plotting library: you keep drawing in matplotlib, ggplot2 or LaTeX, and every figure, from paper to poster, reads as one body of work.
