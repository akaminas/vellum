Drawn by a script, from real data

Each tile is a detail; open it to see the whole figure, with its twin, its PDF and the script that drew it. Static figures come in both twins and follow the theme you choose.

Data: Palmer penguins (Gorman, Williams and Fraser 2014, CC0); Mauna Loa CO₂ (NOAA GML and Scripps, public domain); life expectancy (UN WPP 2024 and others, processed by Our World in Data, CC BY 4.0); natural disasters (EM-DAT via Our World in Data); Auto MPG (UCI); Les Misérables co-occurrence (Knuth 1993); US census by age (IPUMS); driving and petrol prices (Fairfield, NYT 2010); country outlines (Natural Earth). Full list: data/SOURCES.md. Figures built from an analytic function or invented numbers say "illustrative".
